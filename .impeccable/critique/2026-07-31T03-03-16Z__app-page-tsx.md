# Workout-data transfer critique

Dual-agent assessment: independent visual/UX review plus isolated
detector/browser audit, synthesized after the polish pass.

## Verdict

**35/40 — strong preserve-mode operational UI**

The transfer flow is recognizably Setline: a compact training-ledger utility
with device-first language, blue evidence states, explicit destructive
semantics, and no competition with the active workout path.

## Findings

- **P2 — Mobile discovery remains secondary.** At 390px, Workout Data follows
  the seven programme days and Programme Rules. This is intentional for the
  current scope so backup tooling never outranks workout execution, but a
  future account/data surface could expose a faster route.

## Resolved during polish

- Restored keyboard focus to the backup chooser after Cancel or Replace removes
  the preview.
- Added a persistent visible receipt for downloads, cancellation, replacement,
  and queued account synchronization.
- Added current-device active-session and history context beside the incoming
  backup, plus a prompt to download the current backup before replacement.
- Replaced format-led labels with task-led Setline backup language.
- Increased transfer copy/control type and moved error colors into semantic
  tokens.

## Positive findings

- No horizontal overflow at 390, 768, or 1440 pixels.
- Both transfer controls measure 47.9 pixels high at every required width.
- Native buttons, a labelled region, explicit alert/status semantics, and
  defensive value wrapping provide a sound accessibility base.
- The parser, preview, and activation contract remains independent of the UI,
  bounded to 2 MiB, and covered by focused tests.
