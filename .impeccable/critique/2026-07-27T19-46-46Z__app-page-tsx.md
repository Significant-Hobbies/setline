## Design Specificity Verdict

Setline reads as a purpose-built workout execution instrument rather than a
generic dashboard. The ordered session rail, attempt notation, timestamp-derived
rest state, correction workflow, and recorded-versus-calculated labels all serve
the supplied programme. Lime is now reserved for action and current-state
signals instead of filling large decorative areas.

## What’s Working

- The exact written exercise order remains visible and structurally locked.
- Weight, repetitions, duration, and completion-only steps use controls suited
  to their actual tracking mode.
- A completed or skipped step can be corrected from anywhere in the rail while
  the current workout position remains unchanged.
- The correction sheet uses a native modal lifecycle, contains keyboard focus,
  closes with Escape, and restores focus to its originating rail action.
- Mobile, tablet, and desktop evidence shows a coherent hierarchy with no
  verified clipping or horizontal overflow.
- The deterministic detector returned no findings. Browser checks also found no
  P0/P1 overlap, clipping, or semantic defects.

## Remaining Non-blocking Opportunities

- Add fuller RPE/RIR guidance when the product grows beyond execution-first use.
- Consider collapsing distant rail groups during very long sessions only if
  future gym testing shows the complete sequence is too dense.
